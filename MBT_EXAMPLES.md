The seeding release is the newest tag at the brain's first commit. "Before 0.70" means the brain predates the toolkit's first release.

Each brain's names, in the order a comparison against the templates takes them: its lobespace (the hub's, in a multi-lobe brain), the name `create` filled `<Project>` with (its entrypoint's title), and the name it filled `<project>` with (as in its README's "Use <project> knowledge"), then `--lobes` for a multi-lobe brain. Its project repos are the sibling clones that carry its hook; a dash means none is listed.
